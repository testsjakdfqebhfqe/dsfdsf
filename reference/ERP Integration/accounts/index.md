  Manage GL accounts synced from your ERP. Supports hierarchical chart of
  accounts via `erp_parent_reference_id`. Account types include: `bank`,
  `expense`, `expense_category`, `prepayment`, `accounts_payable`, `checking`,
  `credit_card`. Unique constraint: `erp_reference_id` + `type` must be unique
  per integration.
