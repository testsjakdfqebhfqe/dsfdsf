# Overview

Control D is a premium DNS service designed to enhance security, performance, and reliability for organizations. This Support document outlines our commitments to Organization users, detailing the support services provided.

# Definitions

* **P1** (Critical): Complete service outage or severe degradation affecting all or a significant portion of users (e.g., DNS resolution failure across multiple regions).
* **P2** (High): Partial service degradation impacting a subset of users or features (e.g., slow DNS resolution in a specific region or failure of a non-critical feature).
* **P3** (Low): Minor issues with no significant impact on service functionality (e.g., configuration questions, minor bugs, or feature requests).

# Support Services

## Support Channels

Control D provides comprehensive support to Organization users through the following channels:

* **Email Support**: Contact our support team at [help@controld.com](mailto:help@controld.com) for assistance
* **Barry the Chatbot**: Available 24/7 via the Control D dashboard to troubleshoot non-critical issues and provide immediate assistance.
* **Community Channels**:
  * **Reddit**: Engage with other users and our team at [https://www.reddit.com/r/ControlD/](https://www.reddit.com/r/ControlD/) for non-urgent inquiries.
  * **Discord**: Join our community at [http://discord.gg/dns](http://discord.gg/dns) for discussions and support.

## Support Availability and Response Times

* **Email Support**: Available 7 days a week, 9 AM to 9 PM Eastern, for all Organization users.
* **Barry the Chatbot**: Available 24/7 for non-critical (P3) issues.

Response Times:

* P1 (Critical): Initial response within 1 hour via email.
* P2 (High): Initial response within 6 hours via email.
* P3 (Low): Initial response within 24 hours via email or immediate assistance via Barry.

## Support Exclusions

Support does not include:

* Development or debugging of customer applications or configurations.
* Issues caused by customer hardware, software, or network misconfigurations.
* Assistance with third-party services not managed by Control D.

We may assist you with these scopes on a best-effort basis only. 

# Terms

Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings set forth in the Control D Organization Subscription Agreement. This support policy applies only to paid Organization plans and does not cover free, trial or consumer services.

For additional details or to discuss your support package, contact your account manager or email [business@controld.com](mailto:business@controld.com).
