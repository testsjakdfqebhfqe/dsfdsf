This Endpoint Setting, which you can find when editing an Endpoint, enables Analytics on a specific Endpoint. There are 3 levels to choose from:

- **No Analytics** - Disable the Analytics feature on this Device.
- **Some Analytics** - Will only store counts of blocks, redirects and bypasses, but not queries themselves or any additional data.
- **Full Analytics** - Will store DNS queries and other additional metadata to generate complete reports.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/5156d93e46beac2949a19187c28aa2251007531db913d3ce95bab1d688c4e953-analytics-levels.png" align="center" width="100%" />


Once Analytics are enabled on a Device, you can view your stats in the [Analytics section](doc:analytics).

## Storage Region

The very first time you enable Analytics on a Device, you will be asked to pick a Storage Region. This governs where your Analytics data will reside. Choose an appropriate jurisdiction.
