# What are Reports

This section of the Dashboard is available to Organization accounts, and allows you to receive scheduled reports over email that provide you with a summary of activity inside an Organization. 

Report frequency is one of the following:

* Daily
* Weekly
* Monthly

![](https://files.readme.io/0f8f7bdb79ec55e960f16e663c8b355eae2c6c023550dfd5667f41f91fe568f5-image.png)

# Create Report

In your primary Organization, head over to Analytics -> Reports and click the `+` button. Fill out a brief form.

![](https://files.readme.io/4aeb4c9c830b44b7cdc4c2b04d9ee447f31a919231663f57fa727231a8f28b1c-image.png)

You're done. The email addresses you used in this form will get a report every day, week or month.

![](https://files.readme.io/c1287be-image.png)
