## Google Single Sign-On

When creating a new **Personal** account you can choose Google's **SSO** via the **Get Started - Personal** area. Enter your Google/Gmail email address and click **Continue With Google**.

<Image align="center" width="100%" src="https://files.readme.io/1841fe21fcd7e85e8cee918f9c70f05daa816dcdc2a5e9ed20c2903291c14819-passkeys-personal-signup.png" />

After it's configured you'll use the same option on the **Log In** page as well.

<Image align="center" width="100%" src="https://files.readme.io/67b7ecee20b832224f9fd2156d968a17234c5535975471b74e33e3fe34dcb7e9-passkeys-login.png" />

This will initialize the onboarding steps with GoogleSSO

<Image align="center" width="55% " src="https://files.readme.io/9b395715b21140c1a6ceeee214226268af2847d40f3f31791d12148c13c1b185-image.png" />

Once authenticated you'll be logged into the Control D dashboard. In the **Account** area it'll show that you're using Google Single Sign-On. 

<Image align="center" width="55% " src="https://files.readme.io/b2224a498a42276e384fa32ab43763f3d7f802746fd505ca6ce364a6ec1352b0-image.png" />

**NOTE** that since you're using a third-party authentication service, features such as 2FA and others which would normally require a password will not be available. This is because GoogleSSO is handling authentication (thus you haven't supplied our system with a password). If you'd like to convert your account to traditional username/password (and remove SSO) please contact the Support team and we'll get you sorted out.
