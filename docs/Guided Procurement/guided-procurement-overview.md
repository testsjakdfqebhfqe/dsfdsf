The Paylocity for Finance Guided Procurement module provides a collaborative intake process that helps companies ensure every purchase is easy, compliant, and transparent, . Guided Procurement allows you to build a customized and dynamic purchase workflow that directs employees to gather the right information and documentation needed by various stakeholders like IT, procurement, and legal so that all necessary approvals are secured before a purchase is made.

## How Guided Procurement works:

Before spending company money, employees must submit a purchase request on the Airbase Customer web portal.

1. Based on the type and nature of the purchase request, they are automatically guided through
2. milestones to submit the information required by the relevant stakeholders.
3. Once they submit the request, each milestone is routed to the appropriate approvals for review and approval.
4. Once all milestones have been approved in the request, the vendor is paid.<br />

A purchase request includes:

- An intake form capturing basic details around the purchase, which can be customized to capture additional details.
- Custom approval milestones built using no-code tools. Each milestone has its own data form, set of approvers, and conditions.
- A mandatory finance and accounting milestone present in every request, which allows the user to choose between a Purchase Order or a Virtual Card. Additional details and GL coding applicable for the request are captured in this milestone, depending on the selection made by the employee.

Benefits of Airbase Guided Procurement:

1. Guide employees through an automated procurement process that makes it easy to request and get approvals for spend.
2. Control risk: Eliminate compliance leakage across all departments and automate the benefits of a centralized procurement process.
3. Unite stakeholders with a collaborative workflow that integrates with their systems.
4. Get complete and real-time visibility into all the steps of the procurement process within one consistent workflow.
