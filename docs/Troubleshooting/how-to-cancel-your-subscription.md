You can cancel your Control D subscription in a few easy steps.

## Desktop/Tablet

1. Log in to [Control D](https://controld.com/)
2. Go to the Billing area using the navigational menu (click Preferences on the left hand side, and then Billing)\
   ![](https://files.readme.io/0338d7f-image.png)
3. Click the pencil icon in the Subscription Status row
4. Select a reason\
   ![](https://files.readme.io/cbbcba1-image.png)
5. Depending on the option you selected, write a couple of sentences about why you're cancelling so that we can make Control D better for you!\
   ![](https://files.readme.io/0c969af-image.png)
6. Enter your password and click "Deactivate"

That's it! Your payment method of choice will no longer be charged. You will be able to use Control D until your current billing cycle has ended. Come back soon!
